The 3D models in this folder (*.glb) are adapted from the Z-Anatomy atlas
(https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy, by Gauthier Kervyn and Marcin Zielinski),
which is itself derived from BodyParts3D (The Database Center for Life Science, DBCLS).
Changes: selected structures were extracted, merged per structure, clipped, simplified and compressed.
